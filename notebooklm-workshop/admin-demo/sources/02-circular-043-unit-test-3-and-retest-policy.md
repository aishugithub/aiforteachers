### 3. Portions

1. Subject teachers must give the Class 11 UT3 portions to students in writing (school diary) by **Monday, 9 November 2026**, and post them on the school app for parents.

### 4. Question paper submission (subject teachers)

1. **Class 11 UT3:** submit the question paper, answer key with marking scheme, and filled-in blueprint to the Exam Cell (Mr. Senthil Kumar) by **Wednesday, 18 November 2026**.
2. **Class 12 Pre-Board 1:** submit two sets of question papers with marking schemes, following the latest CBSE sample paper pattern, by **Monday, 30 November 2026**.
3. Every question paper must follow the blueprint approved by the department. The marks for each chapter must match the blueprint. Papers that do not match will be returned for correction.
4. At least 20% of marks must be for competency-based questions (case-based / application).

### 5. After the test

1. Class 11 UT3: show corrected answer scripts to students by **Thursday, 10 December 2026**, and enter marks in the ERP by **Friday, 11 December 2026**.
2. Class 12 Pre-Board 1: show scripts by **Monday, 4 January 2027**, and enter marks in the ERP by **Wednesday, 6 January 2027**.

### 6. Re-test policy

1. A re-test is given **only** to students who were absent for **medical reasons**. This applies to unit tests and the Half-Yearly Examination.
2. A **medical certificate** must be submitted to the class teacher **within 3 working days** of the student returning to school.
3. The re-test must be conducted **within 7 days** of the student's return, by the subject teacher. Only **one re-test per subject** per examination is allowed, using a different paper.
4. Absence for any other reason (family functions, travel, coaching-centre tests, competitions not approved by the school) will be marked **AB**, and no re-test will be given.
5. **There is no re-test for Pre-Board examinations.** Class 12 students absent from Pre-Board 1 for medical reasons will be assessed on Pre-Board 2 (January 2027).
6. In exceptional cases, parents may write to the **Principal at least 7 days before the test**. The Principal's decision is final. Class teachers must not promise a re-test to parents.
