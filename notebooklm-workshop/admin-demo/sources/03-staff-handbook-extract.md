### Section 4. Leave for teachers

4.1 Each teacher is entitled to **12 days of Casual Leave (CL)** in an academic year. CL cannot be carried over to the next year.

4.2 CL must be applied for in the ERP **at least one working day in advance**, except in an emergency. In an emergency, inform the Vice Principal by phone before 7:30 am and apply in the ERP on the day you return.

4.3 **Medical Leave (ML)** of up to 10 days per year is available. A medical certificate is required for medical leave of **more than 2 consecutive days**.

4.4 CL will **not** be sanctioned on examination days, the PTM day, or Annual Day, except in an emergency.

4.5 Before going on planned leave, the teacher must leave lesson plans and work for each class with the Substitution In-charge, Mrs. Revathi N.

### Section 5. Examination and invigilation duties

5.1 Invigilators must report to the Exam Cell **30 minutes before** the start of the examination to collect question papers and answer sheets.

5.2 Mobile phones must be switched off and kept in the staff room during invigilation.

5.3 A teacher will **not** invigilate their own class during the paper of the subject they teach to that class.

5.4 Invigilators must mark attendance in the answer-sheet register, collect the signatures of all students present, and return the sealed packet to the Exam Cell within 15 minutes of the test ending.

5.5 Duty swaps are allowed only with the written approval of the Exam Cell In-charge, at least **2 working days** before the duty.

5.6 Relievers stay in the staff room during the test and replace any invigilator who needs to leave the hall.

### Section 6. Attendance of students

6.1 Students must have **at least 75% attendance** to be eligible for the term-end examination. For Class 12, CBSE also requires **75% attendance** for a student to be allowed to sit the board examination; exemptions are given by CBSE only for serious medical reasons and similar cases, with documents.

6.1A Class 12 students must attend school in January and February for practical examinations and Pre-Board 2. Leave for coaching classes or entrance-exam preparation is **not** treated as approved leave.

6.2 At the end of every month, the class teacher must send an **attendance-shortage letter** (Format A6, available in the ERP) to the parents of every student below 75%, and record it in the class register.

6.3 Leave letters from parents must be kept in the class file for the whole academic year.

### Section 7. Communication with parents

7.1 Teachers must communicate with parents **only through the school app, the school diary, or a meeting at school**. Teachers must not create personal WhatsApp groups with parents or share their personal phone numbers.

7.2 Teachers must reply to parent messages **within 2 working days**. If the matter needs the Principal's decision, the teacher should say so and forward it to the office.

7.3 Teachers must **never share another student's marks, rank, behaviour or personal details** with a parent. On request, the class average for a test may be shared, without names.

7.4 Parents who cannot attend the PTM may book a meeting with the class teacher on any working day from **3:30 pm to 4:15 pm**, with an appointment through the school app.

7.5 Messages to parents must be polite, short, and in simple English. A Tamil version may be added where helpful.

### Section 8. Student data and AI tools

8.1 Student names, phone numbers, addresses, health details and family details must **not** be uploaded to any AI tool or website not approved by the school.

8.2 When using approved AI tools for analysis, use **roll numbers only**. Match roll numbers back to names on the school computer.

8.3 Any letter, remark or report drafted with the help of an AI tool must be checked by the teacher before it is sent. The teacher is responsible for its accuracy.
