**Circular No.:** GPS/2026-27/041
**Date:** Thursday, 22 October 2026
**To:** All Class Teachers and Subject Teachers, Classes 11 and 12
**Subject:** Term 1 (Half-Yearly) results, report cards, Parent-Teacher Meeting and CBSE data verification

---

Dear Teachers,

The Term 1 Half-Yearly Examinations for Classes 11 and 12 ended on 9 October 2026. Please complete the following tasks on time so that report cards reach parents at the Parent-Teacher Meeting.

### 1. Term 1 marks entry (all subject teachers)

1. Upload Term 1 (Half-Yearly) marks for every student to the **School ERP portal**. The **last date is Thursday, 5 November 2026, 4:00 pm**. The portal will be locked after this time.
2. Mark absent students as **AB**, not 0. Students who took an approved re-test must have the re-test mark entered with the remark "RT".
3. Show answer scripts to students in class and collect their signatures on the script-viewing sheet before entering marks.

### 2. Report card remarks (class teachers)

1. Enter one descriptive remark per student in the ERP by **Monday, 9 November 2026**. Remarks must be constructive, specific and free of comparisons with other students.
2. Avoid remarks such as "weak", "careless" or "lazy". Use phrases that describe what the student can do next.

### 3. Students needing support (class teachers)

1. Send the list of students who scored **below 40% in any subject** in the Half-Yearly Examination to the Academic Coordinator, Mrs. Latha S, by **Friday, 6 November 2026**. Use roll numbers and subject only.
2. These students will be enrolled in remedial classes (timings will be shared separately).

### 4. Parent-Teacher Meeting (PTM)

1. The PTM for Classes 11 and 12 will be held on **Saturday, 21 November 2026, 9:00 am to 12:30 pm** in the respective classrooms.
2. Class teachers must collect printed report cards from the Exam Cell and check them for errors by **Wednesday, 18 November 2026**.
3. Send PTM time slots (10 minutes per family) to parents through the **school app** by **Monday, 16 November 2026**.
4. Parents of students below 40% in any subject, and of Class 12 students below 75% attendance, should be given the first slots.
5. Keep the PTM attendance register signed by every parent who attends. Submit it to the office on the same day.

### 5. CBSE data verification — Class 12 only

1. Class 12 class teachers will receive a printout of each student's CBSE registration data (name, parents' names, date of birth, subject codes) from the office on **2 November 2026**.
2. Each student and one parent must check and sign the printout. Return all signed printouts to the office by **Friday, 13 November 2026**. Corrections after this date may not be possible.
3. Do not send photos of the printout over messaging apps. The printout contains personal data.

### 6. Practical records — Classes 11 and 12

1. Practical record notebooks and investigatory project files for Physics, Chemistry, Biology and Computer Science must be complete, corrected and signed by the subject teacher by **Tuesday, 15 December 2026**.
2. CBSE Class 12 practical examinations are expected in January 2027. Dates will be shared when CBSE announces them.

Please acknowledge this circular in the ERP.

**Principal**
