Dear Parents,

**Invitation to our 22nd Annual Day — "Roots and Wings"**

We are delighted to invite you to the 22nd Annual Day celebrations of Greenfield Public School.

**Date:** Saturday, 13 December 2025
**Time:** 4:30 pm to 7:30 pm (please be seated by 4:15 pm)
**Venue:** School Open Auditorium

**Chief Guest:** A retired scientist from the Indian Space Research Organisation, who will speak to students about curiosity and hard work.

**The evening will include:**
- Welcome dance by the Primary section
- Prize distribution for academic and sports achievers of 2024-25
- Tamil drama by Classes 6 and 7
- Group dance by Class 11
- Science-themed skit by Class 9
- Vote of thanks by the School Pupil Leader

**Please note:**
1. Each family may bring **two adults**. Entry is by the invitation card sent with your child.
2. Students taking part must report to their classrooms by **3:00 pm** in costume. Make-up will be done at school.
3. Parking is available at the back gate. Please use the front gate for entry only.
4. Photography is allowed from your seat. Please do not stand in the aisles.
5. Students will be handed over to parents at their classrooms after the programme. Please show the invitation card.

We look forward to celebrating our children's talents with you.

Warm regards,

**Principal**
Greenfield Public School
