The Water Cycle

Class 7 Science · Revision page · Your Name, Your School

The four stages

  1. Evaporation: the Sun heats water in seas, rivers and lakes, and it turns into water vapour.
  2. Condensation: the water vapour rises, cools and forms tiny droplets: clouds.
  3. Precipitation: the droplets join, grow heavy and fall as rain, hail or snow.
  4. Collection: the water gathers in seas, rivers, lakes and underground, and the cycle starts again.

Key words

Water vapour
Water in the form of a gas. We can't see it.
Transpiration
Plants giving out water vapour through their leaves.
Groundwater
Rainwater that soaks into the soil and is stored under the ground.

Check yourself

1. Why do wet clothes dry faster on a sunny, windy day?

Heat and wind both speed up evaporation.

2. Why do drops of water form on the outside of a glass of cold water?

Water vapour in the air cools on the glass and condenses.

3. Name one way we can save rainwater at school.

Rainwater harvesting: collect it from the roof into a tank or a recharge pit.
