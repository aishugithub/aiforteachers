## Why this chapter

Electromagnetic Induction (NCERT Class 12 Physics, Chapter 6) comes up in CBSE boards and in JEE/NEET every year. It is formula-heavy and full of classic misconceptions about Lenz's law, which makes it a good showcase for quizzes, flashcards and the audio overview.
